Coming Soon: Art Hall and The Art Districtt

Model Auctions 1 min read Filed under: Coming Soon

The next Model Auctions feature goes behind the runway, to the woman who builds the shows.

HOUSTON — Art Hall is the founder of The Art Districtt, the Houston company behind the No Shade Fashion Show at Sanman Studios in July and Canvas to Catwalk, which returned for its second edition at The Event Place on November 7. She curates the shows, and she choreographs them.

Model Auctions was at both. The next feature here is about her: how the shows come together, the designers, models and artists she puts on one runway, and what she is building in Houston fashion.

Photographs and film from Canvas to Catwalk will run with it.
