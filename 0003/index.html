02The show

Canvas 2 Catwalk: Ode to Basquiat

On November 7, The Art Districtt brought its second Canvas 2 Catwalk to The Event Place in Houston: a night for Jean-Michel Basquiat, with seven designers, live painting, a poet and a ballerina. Model Auctions photographed and filmed it from the first walk to the last.
